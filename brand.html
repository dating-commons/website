Type & tone

Editorial serif for thesis, clean sans for action.

  • Display direction: classic serif, high-trust, civic, warm.
  • Body direction: contemporary sans, readable, direct, un-hyped.
  • Voice: calm, rigorous, public-interest, socially literate.
  • Avoid: startup jargon, therapeutic mush, hookup-brand energy, and NGO vagueness.

Usage

What supporters and partners can do.

Allowed

  • Use the public logos to link to the foundation or initiatives.
  • Use the share cards and copy to spread awareness.
  • Use the mark in event listings, articles, volunteer pages, and partner directories.

Not allowed

  • Do not imply official partnership, endorsement, or chapter status without approval.
  • Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, or redraw the mark.
  • Do not place the logos into crude, scammy, or exploitative dating contexts.
