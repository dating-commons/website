One foundation behind three dating initiatives

A foundation for better ways to meet.

The Dating Commons exists because too much of modern dating has been enclosed by high-friction apps, extractive incentives, and socially dead formats. The Dating Commons is the umbrella behind three public initiatives: The Dating Institute for research, HeartFull for the free dating app experiment, and the Offline Dating Movement for real-world events and chapter building.

The thesis

Connection is a commons, not a premium feature.

Match Group and its peers turned meeting people into a funnel governed by subscriptions, boosts, and algorithmic scarcity. The Dating Commons argues that this is not just a product problem. It is a social-structure problem. We want to restore diversity to how people meet. The Dating Institute publishes the research, HeartFull tests the aligned product path, and the Offline Dating Movement builds real-world local scenes that feel alive again.

Movement initiative

Offline Dating Movement

The Offline Dating Movement is the in-person events initiative of The Dating Commons. It is a distributed network of people organizing offline meetups that feel more natural, more respectful, and more human than apps, awkward speed dating, or loud party energy.

The goal is not just to host events centrally. It is to help local organizers build chapter culture, formats, and member spaces that make real-world connection easier again. Start with the first Sydney event or the host path below.

Movement initiative

The Offline Dating Movement starts with Sydney Offline Dating Club.

Sydney Offline Dating Club is the first public proof of concept for the Offline Dating Movement, the events initiative of The Dating Commons. It is designed for people who want something better than dating apps, awkward speed dating, or loud party energy.

Some events are playful and structured. Some are quieter and more conversational. Some are built around movement, shared activity, or host-guided formats that help people relax enough to show real personality. Registration and host applications route through HeartFull so the online and offline layers stay connected.

Tone rules

Not a hookup group. Not a trashy reality-show vibe. Not a cringe performance space.

The atmosphere should feel warm, socially intelligent, emotionally aware, and fun. Meetup can handle discovery. A private member hub can handle updates, coordination, and safer ongoing contact.

Principles

The operating taste of the foundation.

No paywalls on connection

We do not accept the idea that better human access should be auctioned to the highest bidder.

Warm social design

Formats should lower performance pressure, not intensify it. Tone is part of the product.

Declared bias, sourced claims

We can have a view and still do rigorous work. The obligation is to disclose it and source the argument.

Local scenes matter

The movement is not abstract. It should eventually live in rooms, parks, cafes, walks, and recurring chapter rituals.
